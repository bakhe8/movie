### Permanent appearance evolution (ADR-115)

Owner clarification (2026-09-06): `cinema / premiere / montage` are permanent user-selected experience tracks, not temporary test arms and not candidates for elimination or eventual merger. The saved value follows the track as it evolves; it does not freeze one visual release. Each track advances from a versioned baseline through one scoped candidate at a time: an adopted candidate becomes the next baseline, a rejected candidate alone is removed, and the next idea starts from the latest adopted baseline. `system / light / dark` remains an independent colour preference, and no experiment or default change may overwrite an explicit saved appearance.

Treat the current implemented appearances as `Baseline v1 — owner-approved visual baseline; pre-measurement; per-track structural differentiation not yet established`. Before opening a candidate intended to evolve a baseline, record in its existing task or PR: track, surface and current baseline; protected identity; one hypothesis, exact delta and what stays fixed; formative success signal plus accessibility/performance guardrails; decision owner and point; rollback. This does not apply to regression, conformance or accessibility fixes that restore the accepted baseline. Keep only one active candidate per track. Use `PROPOSED → BUILT → VISUALLY_ACCEPTED → FORMATIVE_REVIEWED → ADOPTED / REJECTED`; a revision returns the same candidate to `PROPOSED`, only `ADOPTED` advances the baseline, and every closed candidate records its evidence and decision before the slot receives a new idea. The first required distinction is structural on a shared core journey (density, hierarchy, action placement, disclosure or navigation); palette-only work counts only for an explicit colour/contrast/accessibility hypothesis.

Before public launch or while eligible human traffic is insufficient, use owner acceptance, task usability, accessibility and formative research without claims of a causal winner. The future event contract is definition-only: `appearance_exposed` means that the resolved track was actually rendered; `appearance_selected` means the first successful persistence of an explicit track; `appearance_changed` means successful persistence from one explicit track to a different one. A saved preference alone does not prove exposure, and a failed or same-value write is not a change. When activated, every event must identify the track and its baseline/candidate version. Do not add production collection or widen the analytics allowlist until matching versioned disclosure, consent and version-aware enforcement are implemented. Do not build allocation, power/sample, survey or dashboard machinery merely for the current baseline.

Before visual work, read `../../docs/THEME_MODES_2026-09-05.md` as ADR-112's historical delivery contract. Its white/blue/lime GPT «طاولة المونتاج» light foundation is now implemented and approved at L0/L1, bounded L2 uses have landed, and the existing near-black/deep-indigo dark mode remains preserved; treat the current tokens as the baseline, not as transitional values. ADR-113 and ADR-115 govern the current permanent appearances independently of `system / light / dark`. Both colour modes use one route/component/state tree: do not fork screens or rebuild shared interaction. Claim exact files in `.claude/SESSIONS.md`; only one owner may change the light-token/manifest/boot bundle at a time, and screen owners consume those tokens without editing that bundle in parallel.
